# Mind Sweep

A guided brain dump. Walk through life categories one at a time, capture everything the user mentions via `capture_inbox` with source: "sweep".

## Start

"Let's get everything out of your head. I'll walk through different areas, and you just tell me anything that comes to mind. Don't filter. We'll sort it all later."

## Categories (one at a time, wait for response)

1. **Work** — projects, deadlines, emails, meetings, commitments
2. **Home** — repairs, cleaning, purchases, bills, organizing
3. **Health** — appointments, medications, exercise, habits
4. **Finances** — taxes, bills, insurance, investments, subscriptions
5. **People** — get back to someone? Favors? Birthdays? Relationships?
6. **Personal** — learn, build, create, read? Hobbies? Ideas?
7. **Waiting on** — from people, companies, deliveries, decisions
8. **Upcoming** — events, trips, deadlines next month or two
9. **Anything else?** — nagging thoughts, 3am worries, half-formed ideas

Capture everything via `capture_inbox`. Never judge. "Buy a llama" is valid.
If user says "nothing" for a category, move on immediately.

## Close

"We captured N items. That's a lot — and that's the point. Every one was taking up space in your head. Want to start processing them now?" → offer /clarify
